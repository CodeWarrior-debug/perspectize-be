       - After onboarding (flag set), clicking the glasses icon opens the picker normally (no skip button, no onDismiss)

    3. **Random defaults on user creation:**
       - In `frontend/src/lib/queries/hooks/useCreateUser.ts`, modify the `onSuccess` callback:
         - After existing cache invalidation and auto-select logic
         - Import `useUpdateGlassesPreference` and `randomGlassesPreference` from types
         - Call `randomGlassesPreference()` to get a random shape + purple color
         - Fire `updatePreferencesMutation.mutate({ userId: newUser.id, shape: randomShape.toUpperCase(), color: 'purple' })`

       - Consider placing the glasses icon and its picker outside the Select component, adjacent to it in the Header
